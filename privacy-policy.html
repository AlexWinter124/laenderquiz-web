Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-31

This Privacy Policy describes how the application "Winter Media Publisher" ("the App"), operated by Winter Media ("we", "us"), handles data in connection with its use of the TikTok Content Posting API.

What the App does

The App publishes short-form country-quiz videos to TikTok creator accounts that we own and operate ourselves (including @laender.quiz and @country.quiz), using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Data we process

  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens for the TikTok accounts we ourselves authorize the App to publish to.
  • Basic account information returned by TikTok's API for those same accounts (e.g. username, display name, avatar), used only to confirm we are publishing to the correct, authorized account.
  • Video files and text (titles, captions) that we create ourselves for publishing.

Data we do not collect

The App does not collect personal data from viewers of the published videos, and is not directed at, and does not knowingly collect data from, any TikTok users other than the accounts we ourselves operate and authorize.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored securely and are not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party, except where required to operate the App (e.g. TikTok's own API) or where required by law.

Retention and revocation

Tokens are retained only for as long as needed to operate the connected accounts. You (or we, as the account owner) can revoke the App's access at any time via TikTok account settings, which immediately stops any further processing.

Your rights

For any question about this policy, or to request information about or deletion of data held by the App, contact us at alex.geoquiz.de@outlook.de.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
